Privacy Policy

App: SmartKids (SmartKids: Learn ABC & 123)

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

Summary for Parents: SmartKids is an educational app designed for children. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information, location data, audio recordings, or personal data of any kind.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the SmartKids mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of children, parents, and educators who use our application.

2. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) Compliance

Our app is designed specifically for children under the age of 13. We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play’s Families Policy:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or addresses.
  • We do not collect persistent identifiers (such as Advertising IDs or IMEI numbers) for behavioral profiling or tracking.
  • We do not track precise or coarse geographical location.
  • We do not have chat rooms, user profiles, or social media integrations.

3. Information We Collect

None. SmartKids operates completely on-device.

  • Local Storage: The app stores basic game progress (such as earned stars, unlocked badges, voice speed preferences, and sound settings) strictly inside the device's local offline memory (localStorage). This data never leaves your device.
  • Speech & Audio: Audio synthesis and pronunciation run on-device using Android's native Text-to-Speech engine and local Web Audio oscillators. No voice data or user audio is ever recorded, captured, or transmitted.

4. Advertising and Analytics

SmartKids contains NO third-party advertisements and NO commercial tracking SDKs. Your child will never see banner ads, video popups, or interstitial ads inside this application.

5. In-App Purchases

The application does not offer any in-app purchases or subscription models. All educational content and games are completely free and accessible without payment barriers.

6. Permissions Requested

The app requires only the standard permissions necessary to display web views and output educational speech audio via the device's speaker. We do not request access to:

  • Camera or Microphone
  • Contacts, SMS, or Phone State
  • Device Storage / Photo Gallery
  • GPS / Location Services

7. Security

Since we do not collect any personal data over the internet, there is no risk of your child's data being compromised on external servers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update our Privacy Policy. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated effective date.
